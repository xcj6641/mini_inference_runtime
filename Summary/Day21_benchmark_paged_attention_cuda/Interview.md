I implemented a correctness-first CUDA PagedAttention path that reads K/V directly from paged cache blocks through a block table, without first reconstructing contiguous K/V tensors. I benchmarked it against the runtime's existing materialize-then-attend path from 128 to 4096 tokens. In this implementation, materialization represented 98.4-99.95% of the component-wise baseline and scaled linearly, reaching about 218 ms at 4096 tokens. Direct paged attention reached about 1.32 ms at 4096 tokens and reduced the measured end-to-end latency by roughly 101-171x. I would not claim that my naive kernel is faster than optimized PyTorch attention itself—it was actually slower when materialization was excluded. The result shows that eliminating an expensive data-layout conversion can matter more than the attention computation, while also exposing the serial reductions, unfused stages, and memory-access optimizations needed for a production kernel.
